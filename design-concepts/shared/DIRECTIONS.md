# The ten creative directions

Each concept gets one direction. The palette and fonts below are **starting points**. You may refine them, but keep the family choices unless there's a strong reason to change, because they are deliberately different across concepts. If you do change a font, pick one that no other direction uses. All fonts come from `shared/fonts.json`.

Concepts must differ in **page composition, typography, palette, imagery treatment and navigation**, not just colour. Respect the "avoid" notes; they protect the other concepts' territory.

## Principles observed in the reference study (hrw.org, October 2026)

These are what the concepts may borrow. Cite 2–3 of them in `concept.json → informedBy`.

- P1. A strong editorial lead with clear scanning hierarchy; asymmetry over equal cards.
- P2. A dated, typed "latest" stream (news strip or list) for recency.
- P3. Hierarchy carried by scale, spacing and separators rather than decorative containers.
- P4. A serif reading face for long-form text, against a sans-serif UI.
- P5. Topic and region taxonomies as cross-cutting entry points (hubs).
- P6. Report covers that set publications apart from news; a filterable research archive.
- P7. An immersive long-report treatment with chapters, summary and downloads.
- P8. Impact framed as problem, action, result and date.
- P9. Trust modules: methodology, finances, corrections and safeguarding.
- P10. Participation placed at meaningful points (a donation band before the footer, preference-based newsletter).
- P11. A mobile pattern: lead image above the headline, compact headline lists, donate always visible in the compact header.
- P12. Captions and credits on documentary imagery.
- P13. Video features with poster and play affordance.
- P14. Annual publications treated as curated objects.

**Never copy the reference.** That means no blue-and-orange brand pairing, no "white headline panel overlapping a large photo" hero, no dark utility row above a blue text nav, and none of its logo, copy, photos or layouts.

## 01 · Editorial Authority (`concepts/01-editorial-authority`)

- **Idea:** a broadsheet front page. Authoritative, dense and impeccably structured, with a dominant lead story and dense supporting coverage.
- **Type:** Newsreader for headlines and reading (600–800 display, 400 text); Libre Franklin for UI, kickers and labels (uppercase, tracked).
- **Palette:** Ink #15171B · Newsprint #F7F4EE · Rule #CFC8BA · Crimson #A4161A (actions) · Slate #24425F (links)
- **Navigation:** a centred nameplate masthead (big wordmark, a date line "Saturday 3 October 2026", the descriptor), with the section nav between double rules underneath. Utility links are small; a crimson Donate button sits top right.
- **Homepage:** a 12-column grid with fine vertical column rules. The lead story has a huge headline across about 7 columns with the image beside or below, plus a timestamped "Latest" column. Then a 4-up news row, a research section (cover plus a two-column list of reports), an "In numbers" typographic box, a three-part "How we work" with roman numerals, campaign and newsletter in ruled boxes, and a dense footer.
- **Imagery:** natural colour, 3:2 crops, visible captions and credits.
- **Signature:** column rules plus a timestamped Latest column.
- **Mobile:** compact masthead, horizontally scrolling section nav, lead image above the headline, Latest as a compact headline list.
- **Interior:** classic newspaper feature: centred title, ruled byline, drop cap, pull quote between rules, key findings in the margin column.
- **Avoid:** rounded corners, colour blocks, dark backgrounds.

## 02 · Human Stories (`concepts/02-human-stories`)

- **Idea:** people-centred storytelling. Warm and intimate, with quotes and testimony leading; the work is explained through people.
- **Type:** Fraunces (soft serif; use the italic for quotes) for headings; Nunito Sans for body and UI.
- **Palette:** Cream #FFF9F2 · Warm sand #F3E7D8 · Clay #B4532A (primary) · Cocoa #3B2A24 (text) · Ochre #E0A54B · Sage #7E8F6F
- **Navigation:** a light, simple top bar with the wordmark left, a few visible items, the rest under a friendly "Menu", and a rounded clay "Give" button.
- **Homepage:** the hero is a large intimate image (window-silhouette or empty-chair) with a big italic quote ("I keep his chair at the table…") leading into the feature. Then a "Stories" section of staggered, zigzag image-and-text pairs with rounded corners and generous space; "How your support helps" in three warm steps; the impact story; a donate block with amount chips; newsletter; a warm footer.
- **Imagery:** warm golden grade, rounded corners (20–28px), some circular crops, soft paper texture.
- **Signature:** quote-led hero and zigzag story pairs.
- **Mobile:** full-bleed image hero with the quote, then story cards in a vertical rhythm.
- **Interior:** an immersive warm hero with the title over the image (bottom left), a narrow reading column, large italic pull quote, gentle side notes.
- **Avoid:** dark mode, condensed type, data tables.

## 03 · Research & Evidence (`concepts/03-research-evidence`)

- **Idea:** a precise, publication-led evidence desk. Report discovery, data, methodology and citations come first; decoration is restrained.
- **Type:** IBM Plex Serif (headings), IBM Plex Sans (UI and body), IBM Plex Mono (metadata, dates, figures).
- **Palette:** White #FFFFFF · Graphite #1E2630 · Fog #EEF1F4 · Line #D5DBE1 · Deep teal #0F6E6E (primary) · Signal amber #F2B33D (highlights and marks)
- **Navigation:** the wordmark left, the primary nav, and a **prominent search field** ("Search research, data and stories"). Donate is an outlined teal button, not shouting.
- **Homepage:** the hero pairs the lead investigation (with its key sample figures) and the *Unequal Ground* report cover. Then a "Research library" with filter chips (Topic, Region, Type, Year) and a table-like list of reports (title, type, date, pages, download); a "Data in focus" module with a clean bar chart (heat readings, labelled as sample data); a compact Latest list with mono dates; a "What changed" structured impact table (sample label); "Our methodology" as numbered steps; a research digest newsletter; a structured footer.
- **Imagery:** minimal. Small images in a teal duotone or grayscale, with charts and covers doing most of the visual work.
- **Signature:** the filterable report library table and a data chart.
- **Mobile:** search first, horizontally scrolling filter chips, report list rows.
- **Interior:** an article with a left sticky "On this page" contents list, numbered footnote markers, key findings as data cards with mono numbers, a methodology note, and a download panel.
- **Avoid:** big emotive hero photos, rounded playful UI, display fonts.

## 04 · Bold Advocacy (`concepts/04-bold-advocacy`)

- **Idea:** campaign-first. Expressive poster typography, decisive contrast, and urgent, unmistakable actions.
- **Type:** Anton (uppercase display); Archivo (body and UI; 800 for subheads).
- **Palette:** Black #0B0B0B · White #FFFFFF · Signal yellow #FFE14D (primary action) · Alarm red #E63322 · Concrete #E9E6E1
- **Navigation:** a thick black bar with the wordmark in Anton, uppercase nav items, and a big yellow DONATE block button.
- **Homepage:** a poster hero with "THE LONG WAIT" at a huge size (140px or more) over a black/yellow duotone image, with the subhead and a red CTA. Then the *Every Child in Class* campaign band with the signature counter and an inline sign-up form (name and email fields, "Add your name"); latest stories as a bold numbered list (01–06) with uppercase headlines; the report as a poster-style cover; impact as massive numerals; a brief "How we work"; a "Get the briefing" newsletter; a black footer.
- **Imagery:** high-contrast duotone (black and yellow) or grayscale with a red overlay; halftone or grain texture; hard rectangular crops.
- **Signature:** poster typography and the inline petition form.
- **Mobile:** a sticky bottom action bar ("Add your name" and "Donate"); stacked huge headlines.
- **Interior:** a poster-style header with an oversized title over a duotone image, a mid-article "Take action" box, and an Anton pull quote on a yellow highlight.
- **Avoid:** serif type, soft pastel colours, rounded corners.

## 05 · Quiet Minimalism (`concepts/05-quiet-minimalism`)

- **Idea:** calm, spacious and typographic. Restraint signals seriousness, and reading comes first.
- **Type:** Cormorant Garamond (display, 400–500, large sizes only); Manrope (body and UI).
- **Palette:** Bone #F6F4EF · Charcoal #262626 · Ash text #5E5A52 · Stone rule #B8B2A6 · Moss #4F5D47 (links and accents) · White #FFFFFF
- **Navigation:** minimal. The wordmark left; a "Menu" text button and an underlined "Donate" text link at right. Show the full menu as a clean overlay **on mobile only**, if at all.
- **Homepage:** a large serif headline with lots of whitespace, plus a modest, non-full-bleed image offset to one side. Latest is a simple numbered typographic list of headlines with dates, without images. Then one understated report feature; impact as a single elegant sentence with the figures inline (plus the sample label); three short trust statements; one support band; a single newsletter line; a minimal text footer.
- **Imagery:** selective (2–3 images on the whole page), desaturated and soft, with generous margins and thin captions.
- **Signature:** whitespace and typographic lists.
- **Mobile:** generous spacing, list-based, menu button only.
- **Interior:** a single centred narrow column with a large serif title, a modest image, generous leading and minimal chrome.
- **Avoid:** heavy colour fields, many images, dense grids.

## 06 · Global Atlas (`concepts/06-global-atlas`)

- **Idea:** geography as the organising idea. Explore the work by region on a map, with topics as a second axis.
- **Type:** Space Grotesk (headings and UI); Literata (reading and body).
- **Palette:** Ocean #0B2340 · Chart sand #EFE8D8 · Topo green #2D7A63 · Coral #EE5D48 (actions and markers) · Ice #CFE1EE · Ink #14202E
- **Navigation:** the header puts "Explore: Regions ▾ / Topics ▾" first, with the other items alongside and a coral Donate button. Use the world map (`shared/images/world-map.svg`, or the country paths JSON) where it helps.
- **Homepage:** the hero is a large map with the 5 regions labelled and marked as areas of work (sample counts such as "18 stories" are fine), and a side panel featuring the lead story. Don't tie the lead story to a named country. Then "Explore by region" (5 region cards with mini-maps); "Latest from the field" with region and topic tags; a "Topics" chip grid; the featured report; impact (for example the 60 countries as map dots, with the sample label); "How we work"; get involved; a footer with region links.
- **Imagery:** landscapes and aerials (valley-dawn, heat-roofs), map overlays, coordinate-style metadata as decoration.
- **Signature:** the map hero and region cards.
- **Mobile:** a region selector (segmented control or dropdown) above the lists, with a simplified map.
- **Interior:** an article with a "Context" sidebar card holding a mini-map, region and topic tags, and "More from this region".
- **Avoid:** dark full-page backgrounds, rounded playful UI.

## 07 · Contemporary Magazine (`concepts/07-contemporary-magazine`)

- **Idea:** varied editorial grids, distinctive art direction and a feature-story rhythm, like a monthly magazine.
- **Type:** Instrument Serif (display, with italics); Instrument Sans (UI and body); Spectral is optional for long reading.
- **Palette:** Cream #FBF6EC · Tomato #E5532D · Cobalt #2A44B8 · Pistachio #CFE6B0 · Ink #141414 · Blush #F4C9BD
- **Navigation:** a centred wordmark with an edition label ("October 2026 edition"), a section row underneath with dot separators, and a tomato Donate pill.
- **Homepage:** a split colour-block hero (a cobalt panel with a huge italic serif headline beside the image). Then an "In this edition" bento grid with varied tile sizes (one tall, one wide, two small) and colour-block frames behind images; a spread for the report (big cover, slight offset); a tomato pull-quote band; a horizontal "Field notes" strip; "By the numbers" with big serif numerals on pistachio (sample label); how we work; donate; newsletter; footer.
- **Imagery:** collage-like crops, varied aspect ratios, colour-block frames, occasional circular cut-outs.
- **Signature:** the bento grid and colour-block spreads.
- **Mobile:** stacked spreads with alternating colour blocks and a horizontal feature strip.
- **Interior:** a magazine feature with a split hero (title on a colour block, image beside), a drop cap, and an italic pull quote spanning columns.
- **Avoid:** column rules and newspaper density, dark mode.

## 08 · Community & Participation (`concepts/08-community-participation`)

- **Idea:** welcoming and action-oriented, with clear ways to give, join, attend and learn. Friendly and highly accessible.
- **Type:** Lexend (headings); Atkinson Hyperlegible (body and UI).
- **Palette:** Soft cream #FFF8EC · Sunflower #FFC93C · Leaf #2E8B57 · Navy ink #1D2B3A · Coral #FF6B57 · Sky #CDE7F7
- **Navigation:** a header with action chips "Give · Join · Attend · Learn", the full nav in a second row or menu, and a Donate button. On mobile, a **bottom tab bar**.
- **Homepage:** a welcoming headline ("Stand with families who are still waiting") with four large action tiles (Give monthly, Add your name, Volunteer, Attend the briefing), each with a simple drawn icon. Then the lead feature as a "Why it matters" card; the event as a date-block card; volunteer roles; "Your support at work" impact (sample label); 3 latest stories; trust as an FAQ ("Where does my money go?", "How do you check facts?", one answered); a friendly newsletter sign-up; footer.
- **Imagery:** bright, rounded frames (16–24px), soft organic blob shapes behind some images, community-table and school-morning.
- **Signature:** action tiles and the bottom tab bar.
- **Mobile:** the tab bar plus stacked action tiles in a 2×2 grid.
- **Interior:** an article with a clear "What you can do" box after the introduction and at the end, large accessible text, and the FAQ.
- **Avoid:** dense text grids, dark mode, condensed display type.

## 09 · Institutional Trust (`concepts/09-institutional-trust`)

- **Idea:** an elegant institutional presence that puts credibility, governance, transparency and measured impact up front.
- **Type:** Libre Caslon Display (headings) with Libre Caslon Text where needed; Public Sans (body and UI).
- **Palette:** Navy #14213D · Ivory #FAF7F0 · Antique gold #A88A55 (rules and icons only, not text) · Burgundy #7A2135 (donate) · Slate #4A5568 (secondary text) · White
- **Navigation:** a navy utility bar (Annual Review 2025 · Funding & finances · Contact), a main nav with mega-menu chevrons, breadcrumbs on interior pages, and a burgundy Donate button.
- **Homepage:** a calm split hero with a serif headline left and a framed image right (thin gold rule). Then a formal "Our impact in 2025" stat panel with fine rules and the "Sample figures for illustration" footnote; an "Accountability" document index (annual accounts PDF, methodology, corrections, safeguarding) styled like a register; latest research in three formal cards; an *Annual Review 2025* promo band; a monthly giving band; a formal footer with the registration line.
- **Imagery:** restrained, framed with thin borders, landscapes and architecture (valley-dawn, city-dusk), muted grade.
- **Signature:** the document register and the footnoted stat panel.
- **Mobile:** a 2×2 stat panel, an accordion for documents, utility links in the menu.
- **Interior:** a formal article with breadcrumb and an "About this research" sidebar (methodology, dates, authors, download, corrections contact).
- **Avoid:** loud colour, playful shapes, oversized poster type.

## 10 · Digital Documentary (`concepts/10-digital-documentary`)

- **Idea:** cinematic, immersive investigations with chapters, film and full-bleed imagery, balanced with accessible reading.
- **Type:** Barlow Condensed (display, uppercase with tracking); Source Serif 4 (reading); Barlow (UI).
- **Palette:** Night #0D0F12 · Charcoal #1A1D22 · Bone #EDE7DD (text on dark, and the light reading background) · Amber #F2A93B (accents) · Ember #C8442F (donate) · Smoke #9AA0A8 (secondary text)
- **Navigation:** a slim transparent overlay nav on the full-bleed hero (light text), a "Watch" affordance, and an ember Donate button.
- **Homepage:** a full-bleed cinematic 21:9 hero (vigil-candles or empty-chair) with an "Investigation" label, the title, and a "Watch the short film (4 min)" play button with a timeline. Then a chapter strip (01 The arrest · 02 The search · 03 The silence · 04 What families ask); latest stories as cinematic dark cards; the report as a full-width band; impact as amber numerals on dark (sample label); "How we verify" with icons; newsletter; a dark footer.
- **Imagery:** letterboxed cinematic crops, film grain, dark gradient scrims, captions.
- **Signature:** the film-led hero and chapter navigation.
- **Mobile:** a full-height image hero, chapters as a vertical timeline, a sticky "Watch" button.
- **Interior:** a full-bleed title image, a chapter progress bar, a **light bone reading section** for long text (for accessibility), full-bleed image breaks, and a pull quote over the image.
- **Avoid:** light-only pages, rounded playful UI, dense news grids.
