# Shared client brief and core content (provisional)

Every concept uses this brief and this content, so the client can compare the ten designs fairly. **Use the strings below verbatim** where they appear in a design (headlines, deks, labels, figures, dates). Concepts differ in layout, typography, colour, imagery treatment, navigation and module emphasis, not in facts.

The client hasn't yet supplied its name, logo, languages or page list. Everything here is a **clearly labelled provisional brand with realistic sample content**. It is not a set of client facts. Never present any figure, outcome, name or quotation as real.

---

## 1. Provisional brand

- **Name:** Daybreak Rights Network (short form: Daybreak). This is a *provisional name* standing in for the client's own.
- **Logo:** the client hasn't supplied one. Each concept sets the provisional wordmark "Daybreak Rights Network" in its own typography. A simple abstract symbol is allowed (for example a horizon line with a rising half-circle), but keep it simple and **don't present it as a finished logo**.
- **Descriptor line:** "Independent research and action for human rights"
- **Mission (sample):** "Daybreak Rights Network documents human rights conditions, stands with affected communities, and campaigns for accountability and lasting change."
- **Web address (placeholder):** daybreak.example (use for URLs in browser bars). **Email:** hello@daybreak.example
- **Language:** English only. Don't add language switchers or other languages.

## 2. Audiences (in priority order)

1. Supporters and donors (individuals giving once or monthly)
2. People affected by abuses and the communities who support them
3. Journalists, researchers and policymakers who need credible evidence
4. Volunteers, partners and event attendees

## 3. Website goals

1. Publish credible research and stories that are easy to find by topic and region.
2. Turn readers into supporters through donations, newsletter sign-ups and campaign actions.
3. Build trust by showing how the work is done, what changed, and how the organisation is funded.

## 4. Site map (required pages)

## 16. Interior page: feature story (same page type for all concepts)

The interior mockup is the **feature story page** for "The Long Wait". It includes:
breadcrumb (Home / News & Stories / Detention & Justice), kicker, headline, dek, byline/date/reading time, share actions, a lead image with caption and credit, and a reading column with the body below. It may add a sidebar, pull quote, key-findings box, related report card, support or donate block, related stories, and a footer (a compact footer is fine).

**Body copy (sample, use in this order; shorten if space requires, but keep it coherent):**

1. "Every Sunday, Amira sets one more cup on the table. Her brother was detained at a checkpoint in March 2023. Since then, she has written to four government offices, two prosecutors and one prison administration. None has confirmed where he is held, or whether he is alive."
2. "Amira's family is one of 64 we interviewed for this investigation. Their accounts follow a pattern: an arrest without a warrant, a transfer between facilities, and then silence. Officials told several families that their relatives had 'never been registered'."
3. **Subheading:** "A pattern of silence"
4. "Under international law, a disappearance continues for as long as the fate and whereabouts of the person remain unknown. Each unanswered letter is not only a failure of administration; it extends the harm to the whole family."
5. **Pull quote:** "I keep his chair at the table. One day someone will tell me where he is."
6. **Key findings box (sample figures):** 64 families interviewed · 37 written requests with no reply · 11 detention sites named by witnesses
7. "We shared our findings with the relevant ministries in August 2026 and asked for a response to specific cases. At the time of publication, we had not received one."
8. **Subheading:** "What families are asking for"
9. "The families' requests are specific: a central register of everyone detained, the right to be notified within 48 hours of an arrest, and independent access to all places of detention."

**End-of-article modules (choose at least 2):** related report card (*Unequal Ground* or *No Safe Harbour*), "Support independent research" donate block, newsletter sign-up, 2–3 related stories from §9.
