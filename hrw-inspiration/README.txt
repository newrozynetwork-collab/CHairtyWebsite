HRW WEBSITE DESIGN REFERENCE — 4 OCTOBER 2026

Start with REPORT.html for the gallery or REPORT.txt for plain text.
Give AI-HANDOFF.txt to the next AI together with this folder or the ZIP.

Included:
- A detailed original design analysis and adaptation guidance.
- 500 successful HTML URL retrievals (497 distinct final URLs after redirects).
- 10,711 discovered URLs, with labels and discovery sources.
- 39 selected page/viewport captures, plus 5 expanded navigation states.
- 83 original JPEG captures (full-page and viewport images).
- 270 overlapping slices for reading long captures without downscaling.
- CONTACT-SHEET.jpg for a quick visual overview.
- Measured font stacks, sizes, colors, heading geometry and image-load counts.
- Accessible page structures with narrative prose omitted.
- Page-by-page crawl metadata, inferred template families, source links, and coverage log.
- The scripts used for crawl discovery, report generation, and image preparation.

This is NOT a complete archive or a screenshot of every individual URL.
The crawl stopped at 500 requests with 7,061 eligible URLs still queued.
The true archive size is unknown. External destinations were inventoried but not crawled.
No forms were submitted or donation transactions performed.
Some lazy-loaded/hidden assets, videos, carousels, and animated counters may differ from
their live appearance. See measured load counts and REPORT.txt for limitations.
